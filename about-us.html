Our Services

What we can do for you and company.

High Speed Internet Access

High Speed Internet Access for hotels: wired and Wi-Fi coverage built with Ruckus, UniFi, MikroTik and TP-Link Omada equipment. We survey the property, install it, and support it afterwards.

Grandstream UCM IP PBX

An on-premises IP PBX that sits in your building, so internal calling between rooms and desks keeps working even if the internet connection drops. Staff mobiles can run as extensions with transfer, voicemail and conferencing, and it connects to the analog handsets already wired into your rooms through a gateway, so an older property does not need recabling.

Low Cost Cabling On your Budget

Our networking engineers specialize in customizing IT infrastructures. They can design, install, and support your specific data and voice cabling needs to create a reliable, flexible, and affordable network for your company. If you have a current infrastructure that needs revamping, we can manage that as well. We can re-design your current network so the new equipment runs on it properly.
