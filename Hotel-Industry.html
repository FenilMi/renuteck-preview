What changing over actually involves

  • We install it

    Handsets, cabling and the phone system itself, fitted by our own team. We survey the property first so the quote matches the building rather than a guess.

  • You keep your numbers

    We handle the porting. The number on your signage, your listings and every booking site stays exactly as it is.

  • The phones in your rooms can stay

    If your rooms are already wired for analog handsets, a gateway connects them to the new system. You are not obliged to replace every phone in the building to move to VoIP.

  • PMS integration

    Integration with your property management system is available, so the front desk is not keying the same thing into two screens. Tell us which system you run and we will confirm what it supports.

  • Room and housekeeping features

    Wake-up calls, room status from the handset, check-in and check-out, and room-to-room call blocking — the things a front desk uses every shift.

  • Support afterwards

    The same family-run team that fitted it answers when you call. You are not passed to a call centre that has never seen your property.
