Keep your PBX. Replace the phone lines.

SIP trunking carries your calls over your internet connection instead of copper lines, and connects to the PBX you already own. You keep your existing numbers and handsets. Lines are unlimited, so a full house calling at once will not produce a busy tone, and calls to the USA and Canada are unlimited.
