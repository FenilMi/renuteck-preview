Wi-Fi that holds up when the property is full.

Most guest Wi-Fi complaints are not about the internet line — they are about coverage and capacity. We survey the building, place access points where the guests actually are, and size the network for a full house rather than an empty lobby. We work with Ruckus, UniFi, MikroTik, TP-Link Omada and Grandstream equipment, and with Nomadix for guest access.

Coverage where the guests actually are

We walk the property before quoting: lobby, corridors, guest rooms, meeting space and the areas people actually sit in. Access points go where they are needed rather than where the cabling happens to run.

Sized for a full house

A network that copes with an empty lobby will not cope with a full property on a Friday night. We size the equipment and the internet line for the number of rooms and devices you actually have, not the minimum that will pass a walk-through.

Equipment we install and support
  • UniFi / Ubiquiti: access points, switches and gateways managed from one controller, so adding a floor later does not mean starting again.
  • TP-Link Omada: a lower-cost controller-managed range that suits smaller properties and offices without giving up central management.
  • Ruckus: built for places where a lot of devices share the same space — a full lobby, a meeting room, a conference floor.
  • Nomadix: guest internet gateways that handle login, terms acceptance and per-device bandwidth limits, so one guest cannot take the whole property's bandwidth.
  • Grandstream: routers and access points from the same maker as the phones we fit, which keeps the equipment list and the support call short.
