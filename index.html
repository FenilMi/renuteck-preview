About ReNu Teck

One local team for your phones, network and cameras

ReNu Teck is a family-owned company based in Richardson, Texas, working with hotels, motels, clinics and offices since 2007. We install and support phone systems and Cloud PBX, SIP trunking, guest Wi-Fi and internet, CCTV and structured cabling — across Texas, Oklahoma, New Mexico, Ohio, Arkansas, Hawaii and Louisiana.

Because the same team handles all of it, there is no finger pointing between vendors when something stops working. You call us, and we come and fix it.

Offices and clinics get the same treatment as our hotel work: a phone system sized to your headcount, a network that holds up when everyone is on it, cameras where you need them, and the cabling to tie it together. One team, one number to call.

Guest Internet Solutions

Slow Wi-Fi is one of the things guests reliably mention in reviews. We survey the property, place access points where guests actually are, and size the network so streaming, video calls and a full house at 8pm all still work. Contact us for a free diagnostic and estimate.

Security Camera System

We install UniFi and Uniview (UNV) IP cameras. Monitor entrances, parking areas, hallways and shared spaces, review footage remotely from a phone or computer, and get help with camera placement, installation and ongoing maintenance.

The right camera depends on the space. We survey the property and specify the type that suits each area.
